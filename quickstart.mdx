By the end of this page you will have a connected channel and a post scheduled through seenpaid. Revenue attribution is optional and comes later, if you want it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account" icon="user-plus">
    Sign up at [seenpaid.com/signup](https://seenpaid.com/signup). You then pick a plan and save a card to start a **7-day free trial**. Nothing is charged today; the first charge is on day 8 unless you cancel before then.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how you'll use it" icon="route">
    The setup asks **How will you use seenpaid?** Pick **Scheduling** (the default) or **Scheduling + revenue**. This only sets the revenue-attribution switch; you can flip it later in **Settings → Attribution**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a channel" icon="share-nodes">
    Connect at least one social account. **LinkedIn, Mastodon, Tumblr, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok and Pinterest** connect by signing in on the platform; **Bluesky, Discord, Telegram, Slack, WordPress, Ghost, Dev.to, Hashnode, Medium, Lemmy, Nostr and Webhook** connect by pasting a credential the platform gives you; **X** connects with your own X developer keys. See [Supported platforms](/guides/platforms) for status and caveats per network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and schedule a post" icon="paper-plane">
    Open **New post**, write your caption, add media if you like, pick channels, then **Publish** now or pick a time. Your links go out exactly as you wrote them unless revenue attribution is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results" icon="chart-simple">
    Once it's out, **Results** shows views, likes, comments and shares per post from every platform that reports them. See [Engagement metrics](/guides/engagement) for which ones do.
  </Step>
</Steps>
