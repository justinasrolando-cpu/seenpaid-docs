By the end of this guide you'll have a post published through seenpaid, a tracked link live, and Stripe wired up so the next sale shows up against the right post.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account" icon="user-plus">
    Sign up at [seenpaid.com](https://seenpaid.com/signup). You start on a free trial — no card required to look around.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a channel" icon="share-nodes">
    Go to **Connections** and link at least one social account (X, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Bluesky, or Facebook). This is the account seenpaid will publish to.

    See [Connect your channels](/guides/connect-channels) for the per-platform details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Stripe" icon="stripe">
    In **Connections → Stripe**, click **Connect Stripe** and authorize. This is what lets seenpaid see sales and match them to posts.

    <Warning>
      Without Stripe connected, seenpaid still publishes and tracks clicks — but it can't attribute revenue. Connecting Stripe is what turns clicks into dollars.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish a tracked post" icon="paper-plane">
    Open **Compose**, write your post, include your product link, pick your channel(s), and publish. seenpaid automatically swaps in a tracked short link (`go.seenpaid.com/r/...`) so every click is attributed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the money loop close" icon="chart-line">
    When someone clicks your post and buys, the sale appears in **Revenue** — matched to that exact post. Your top earners rise to the top on their own.
  </Step>
</Steps>
