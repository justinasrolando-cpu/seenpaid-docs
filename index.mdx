title: "What is seenpaid?"
description: "Revenue attribution for your social posts — see which posts actually made money."
---

Every scheduler tells you likes, reach, and clicks. **None of them tell you which post made you money.** seenpaid does.

You connect your social accounts and your Stripe, publish posts through seenpaid, and every sale gets matched back to the exact post that drove it. Not guesses, not last-touch hand-waving — the actual post, the actual dollars.

<Note>
  **The one line:** Dub does it for links, DataFast does it for pages — seenpaid does it for **posts**.
</Note>

## The money loop

seenpaid closes the loop between what you post and what you earn:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Post">
    Publish to X, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Bluesky and more — from the dashboard, or straight from your AI agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track">
    Every post gets a tracked link. Clicks are attributed to the source post automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match">
    When a click turns into a Stripe sale, seenpaid matches the revenue back to the post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Know">
    Your dashboard shows earnings per post, top earners, and revenue by platform — in real dollars.
  </Step>
</Steps>
