---
title: "What is seenpaid?"
description: "A social media scheduler with a built-in AI agent. Schedule to 21 platforms, read engagement back, and optionally see which posts made money."
---

seenpaid is a **social media scheduler**. You connect your accounts, write once, and publish or schedule to 21 platforms from one composer, one calendar, or your AI agent.

Two things set it apart:

- **It is agent-native.** A built-in MCP server with 50 tools lets Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or any MCP client schedule, check and analyse your posts by asking.
- **Revenue attribution is available as an optional add-on.** Turn it on and seenpaid matches sales from Stripe (and seven other payment tools) back to the exact post that drove them. It is off by default; the scheduler is complete without it.
