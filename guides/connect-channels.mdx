A **channel** is a social account seenpaid can publish to. Connect one to start; connect several to cross-post and compare which network actually drives revenue. This is the reference version — for the guided first-time walkthrough, see [Connect your first channel](/start/connect-your-first-channel).

## Two connect methods

You never register seenpaid anywhere. What differs per network is where the permission comes from:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Click &amp; authorize (OAuth)" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square">
    Click **Connect**, sign in on the platform's own site, approve. Nothing to paste.

    **LinkedIn · Mastodon · Pinterest · Tumblr**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paste your own key (BYOK)" icon="key">
    The platform gives *you* a credential — app password, API key, bot token, or webhook URL — that you paste once.

    **Bluesky · Discord · Telegram · Slack · WordPress · Ghost · Dev.to · Hashnode · Medium · Lemmy · Nostr · Webhook**
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channels">
    In the dashboard's left rail, click **Channels**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a platform">
    Click **Connect** (authorize on the platform) or paste the key it shows a field for. seenpaid stores any credential encrypted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it's active">
    The account appears in your channel list as **active** — available in the composer and to your AI agent. Connect as many accounts as your plan allows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's ready today

<Note>
  **Live now:** LinkedIn, Mastodon, Pinterest, Tumblr, Bluesky, Discord, Telegram, Slack, WordPress, Ghost, Dev.to, Hashnode, Medium, Lemmy, Nostr, Webhook.
  **Not yet:** Facebook, Instagram, Threads and TikTok are pending platform review; **X** connects with your own keys but needs your own paid X API credits; **Reddit** and **VK** are coming soon. The dashboard always shows each platform's real state.
</Note>

For every channel with its caption limit, media rules, connect method, and per-platform catches, see [Supported platforms](/guides/platforms).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My account shows as 'needs reconnecting'">
    Access tokens can expire or be revoked by the platform. Open **Channels**, find the account, and click **Reconnect** to re-authorize. It won't publish until reconnected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A platform isn't showing a Connect button">
    It's one of the not-yet-available ones above. Start with any Live channel — the rest light up automatically as review clears or credentials are added.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It's asking for a key I don't have">
    That's the paste-your-own-key path — the platform issues the key, not seenpaid. Grab it from that platform's settings (it's one value) and paste it back in Channels. [Connect your first channel](/start/connect-your-first-channel#method-b-paste-your-own-key) lists where each one lives.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
