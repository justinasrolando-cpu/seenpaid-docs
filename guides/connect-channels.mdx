A **channel** is a social account seenpaid can publish to. Connect one to start; connect several to cross-post. For the guided first-time walkthrough see [Connect your first channel](/start/connect-your-first-channel).

## Three connect methods

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Sign in and authorize" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square">
    Click **Connect**, sign in on the platform's own site, approve.

    **LinkedIn · Mastodon · Tumblr · Pinterest · Facebook · Instagram · Threads · TikTok**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paste a credential" icon="key">
    App password, API key, bot token or webhook URL, pasted once.

    **Bluesky · Discord · Telegram · Slack · WordPress · Ghost · Dev.to · Hashnode · Medium · Lemmy · Nostr · Webhook**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your own X keys" icon="x-twitter">
    **X** connects with the four keys from your own X developer app. X bills API posting to that account.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channels">
    In the left rail, click **Channels**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a platform">
    Click **Connect** (authorize on the platform) or paste the credential it asks for. Credentials are stored encrypted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it's active">
    The account appears as **active**, available in the composer and to your AI agent. Connect as many as your plan allows (5, 15 or unlimited).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's ready today

All 21 platforms can be connected. A few carry conditions:

- **Instagram and Facebook** work only for accounts added to seenpaid's Meta app, because the app has not passed Meta App Review.
- **Pinterest** connects, but publishing currently fails: the Pinterest app is on Trial access, which cannot create Pins in production.
- **TikTok** delivers to your TikTok drafts (inbox), not directly to your profile, and the caption is written in the TikTok app.
- **X** needs your own developer keys and X's own API pricing applies.
- **Reddit and VK** are not available yet.

The per-platform table, limits and caveats are in [Supported platforms](/guides/platforms).

## Account groups

If you post to the same set of accounts often, group them. Groups appear in the composer's channel picker so one click selects the whole set.

## Reconnecting

You should rarely have to. Per platform:

- **Facebook and Instagram**: the Page token seenpaid stores doesn't expire. No reconnecting.
- **Threads and Bluesky** renew themselves in the background. If a Bluesky session lapses, seenpaid signs in again with your app password.
- **LinkedIn** is the exception. LinkedIn's standard grant lasts 60 days and offers no refresh, so LinkedIn needs a reconnect every 60 days. seenpaid emails you about a week before expiry with a reconnect link, and the channel keeps working until it has actually expired.
- **Everything else** rotates its own tokens on a background sweep.

A publish that hits an expired token refreshes it once and retries. Only when the platform actually refuses the refresh (a revoked or expired grant) does the account flip to **needs reconnecting**, with an email and a **Reconnect** button under Channels; a platform outage, rate limit or timeout never does. Reconnecting re-runs the normal connect flow and updates the existing account in place (same account, same history, same scheduled posts). Nothing is duplicated.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A platform isn't showing a Connect button">
    It's Reddit or VK, which aren't available yet. Every other platform is connectable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It's asking for a key I don't have">
    That's the paste-a-credential path: the platform issues the key, not seenpaid. [Connect your first channel](/start/connect-your-first-channel#method-b-paste-a-credential) lists where each one lives.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I hit my account limit">
    New connections are blocked at your plan's cap (Solo 5, Multi-Brand 15). Accounts already connected keep working if you downgrade. Upgrade under **Settings → Billing**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
