A **channel** is a social account seenpaid can publish to. Connect one to start; connect several to cross-post and compare which network actually drives revenue.

## Connect an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    In the dashboard, go to **Connections**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a platform">
    Choose the network you want to link and click **Connect**. You'll be sent to that platform to authorize seenpaid, then returned to the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it's active">
    The account appears in your channel list with an **active** status. It's now available in Compose and to your AI agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bring your own API keys (BYOK)

Some platforms — notably X — require their own API access on paid tiers. Where a platform needs your own credentials, seenpaid supports **bring-your-own-keys**: paste your app keys once in Connections and seenpaid publishes on your behalf.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My account shows as disconnected">
    Access tokens can expire or be revoked by the platform. Open **Connections**, find the account, and click **Reconnect** to re-authorize.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A platform isn't letting me connect">
    A few networks require you to register a developer app on their side first. If the dashboard prompts for API keys, follow the linked steps for that platform, then return and connect.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
