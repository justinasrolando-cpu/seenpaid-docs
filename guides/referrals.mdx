The **Refer & earn** program gives you a share link, credits you a free month when someone you referred becomes a paying subscriber, and adds milestone bonuses as your referrals stack up.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your link">
    Open **Settings → Refer & earn**. Your share link is created the first time you visit; it looks like `seenpaid.com/?ref=YOURCODE`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    When someone signs up through your link, they're tracked as your referral.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn when they subscribe">
    When a referred account becomes a paying subscriber, you get **one free month** and they get a welcome discount on their first invoice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The milestone ladder

| Converted referrals | Reward |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | 1 free month |
| 3 | 3 free months |
| 5 | 6 free months |
| 10 | 1 year free |
| 25 | Lifetime, on us |

The per-referral months and the milestone bonuses stack. The page shows your progress to the next milestone and your months earned so far.

## Fair by design

- **One referral per new account**; self-referrals don't count.
- **Refunds reverse the reward**: if a referred subscriber refunds or charges back, that conversion and its month are voided. Milestones already reached aren't clawed back.
- Rewards are triggered by real, paid conversions verified through billing, never by signups alone.
