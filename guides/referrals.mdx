If seenpaid earns you money, telling other people is worth something. The **referrals** program gives you a share link, credits you free months when someone you referred becomes a paying subscriber, and stacks milestone bonuses as your referrals add up.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your link">
    Open **Referrals** in the dashboard. seenpaid creates your unique share link the first time you visit — it looks like `seenpaid.com/?ref=YOURCODE`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Send it to people who'd get value from seenpaid. When someone signs up through your link, they're tracked as your referral.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn when they subscribe">
    When a referred account becomes a paying subscriber, you get **one free month**, and they get a welcome discount on their first invoice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The milestone ladder

On top of the per-referral month, you cross bonuses as your total converted referrals grow:

| Converted referrals | Reward |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | 1 free month |
| 3 | 3 free months |
| 5 | 6 free months |
| 10 | 1 year free |
| 25 | Lifetime, on us |

<Info>
  Rewards are cumulative — the per-referral months and the milestone bonuses stack. The dashboard shows your progress to the next milestone, your months earned so far, and a leaderboard of top referrers (with "You" highlighted).
</Info>

## Payout preference

You can set whether rewards come as **account credit** (free months applied to your subscription) or **cash**, from the Referrals page.

## Fair by design

The program is built to reward genuine referrals, not gaming:

- **One referral per new account** — a referee can only be attributed once, and self-referrals don't count.
- **Refunds reverse the reward** — if a referred subscriber refunds or charges back, that conversion and its per-referral reward are voided, so a refund-farmed referral can't keep its bonus. (Milestone trophies you've already earned aren't clawed back.)

<Note>
  Rewards are triggered by real, paid conversions verified through billing — never by sign-ups alone. That keeps the program honest, which is the same principle the whole product runs on.
</Note>
