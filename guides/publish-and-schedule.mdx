Composing in seenpaid works like any good scheduler — with one difference: every post you send carries a tracked link, so it's attributable from the moment it goes live. This guide covers the composer in depth: media, per-platform captions, validation, the calendar, and smart scheduling.

## Compose a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the composer">
    From **Posts**, click **New post**. Write your caption.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add media">
    Attach an image or video. You can upload a file, pull one in from a public URL, or generate an image from a text prompt. Some platforms require media — Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest reject text-only posts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your link">
    Paste your product or landing-page URL in the caption. seenpaid swaps it for a tracked short link at publish time, so you don't think about it. (On Instagram and TikTok the tracked link goes on your [bio page](/guides/bio-page) instead, since caption links there aren't clickable.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your channels">
    Select one or more connected accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish or schedule">
    Send now, or pick a future time to queue it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tailor the caption per platform

One idea rarely fits every platform the same way. Where it matters, edit the caption per channel — for example a punchy 280-character version for X and the full write-up for LinkedIn. You send one post; each platform gets its own native wording.

<Tip>
  X's 280 characters is almost always the binding limit when cross-posting. Write the full version for the roomy platforms and a trimmed one for X.
</Tip>

## Post in multiple languages

Selling into more than one country or time zone? In the composer's **Customize & translate per account** panel, each account gets a language dropdown and a **Translate** button. Write your caption once, pick a language for an account, and seenpaid renders it in that language — reaching a Spanish, Brazilian, or Japanese audience each in their own words, from a single post.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write the base caption">
    Compose your post normally, in your own language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a language per account">
    Open **Customize & translate per account**, and for each connected account pick its target language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Translate">
    Hit **Translate** on an account and seenpaid fills its caption with the translation — keeping @mentions, #hashtags, URLs and emoji intact, and fitting the platform's character limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    Every translation stays fully editable — tweak anything, then publish. Each account goes out in its own language, and each still carries its tracked link, so revenue attributes exactly as normal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Translation uses seenpaid's built-in AI layer. The translated caption is just that account's caption — it publishes, attributes, and recycles like any other. To reach the *same* platform in several languages, connect a separate account per language (e.g. a Spanish and an English Instagram).
</Note>

## Validate before you send

Before publishing one caption to several platforms, you can **dry-run** it — check whether it would publish everywhere *without* actually sending. seenpaid reports, per platform, whether it would go out and exactly why not: too long by N characters, media required and none attached, or an account that needs reconnecting. Fix the problems, or drop the failing platforms, before you spend a real publish attempt.

<Info>
  This matters most on Instagram, where a failed publish quietly costs a post slot and is invisible until someone checks. Validating first turns "why didn't this post" into a clear answer up front.
</Info>

## Publish now vs. schedule

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Publish now" icon="paper-plane">
    Sends immediately to every selected channel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Schedule" icon="calendar">
    Queues the post for a future time. Scheduled posts publish automatically — you don't need to be online.
  </Card>

## The calendar

Manage everything from the **Calendar** view. See what's queued across the week, and **drag a post to a new slot** to reschedule it. Draft and scheduled posts can be edited (caption or time) up until they publish; once a post is out, the platforms already have it and it can't be edited — repost a new version instead.

## Let seenpaid pick the slot

Not sure when to post? seenpaid can suggest the next slot — it picks the soonest open time on the **weekday that has actually earned your account the most money**, skipping slots already taken. Before you have enough revenue history to have an opinion, it simply offers the next open slot a few hours out and says so.

## Schedule a whole calendar at once

Planning a week of promos or a thread split across days? You can schedule many posts in one go. Each is scheduled independently — if one fails (say, a caption too long for a platform), the rest still go through and the failure is reported for just that item, so a single mistake never discards the whole plan.
