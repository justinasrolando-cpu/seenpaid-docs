Composing in seenpaid works like any good scheduler — with one difference: every post you send carries a tracked link, so it's attributable from the moment it goes live.

## Compose a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Compose">
    Write your caption. Add media if you want — images and video are supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your link">
    Drop in your product or landing-page URL. seenpaid automatically swaps it for a tracked short link at publish time, so you don't have to think about it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your channels">
    Select one or more connected accounts. You can tailor the caption per platform where it matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish or schedule">
    Send it now, or pick a date and time to queue it. Scheduled posts publish automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Publish now vs. schedule

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Publish now" icon="paper-plane">
    Sends immediately to every selected channel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Schedule" icon="calendar">
    Queues the post for a future time. Manage everything from the **Calendar** view.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Tracked links are automatic

You never paste a tracked link yourself. When a post publishes, seenpaid rewrites your URL to `go.seenpaid.com/r/...` and starts recording clicks against that post. This is what makes revenue attribution possible — see [How attribution works](/how-attribution-works).
