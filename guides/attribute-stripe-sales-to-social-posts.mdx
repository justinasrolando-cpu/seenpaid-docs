title: "How to attribute Stripe sales to social posts"
description: "Match every Stripe payment back to the exact social post that drove it — the tracked-link + pixel method, explained."
---

**Short answer:** put a tracked link on each post, drop a lightweight pixel on your checkout page, and pass the click's tracking id into Stripe as `client_reference_id`. When the payment lands, you match `client_reference_id → click → post`. seenpaid does all of this automatically.

## Why it's hard by default

Every social platform and scheduler shows you **likes, reach, and clicks**. None of them can tell you **which post produced a paying customer**, because the sale happens on a different surface (Stripe) than the post (X, TikTok, Instagram…). The two systems never talk to each other, so the link between "this post" and "this dollar" is lost.

To recover it, you need one identifier to survive the whole journey:

```
post → link click → your site → Stripe checkout → payment
```

## The method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give each post a tracked link">
    Instead of your bare URL, publish a unique short link per post (e.g. `go.seenpaid.com/r/abc123`). The id in that link is what ties a future sale back to this specific post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the click and carry the id forward">
    When someone taps the link, log the click against the post and stash the tracking id — as a first-party cookie and/or a URL parameter — so it's still around when they reach checkout. No third-party cookies needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass the id into Stripe">
    Attach the tracking id to the Stripe payment as **`client_reference_id`**. For Payment Links and Buy Buttons this can be automatic; for a server-side Checkout Session you set one field:

    ```js
    const session = await stripe.checkout.sessions.create({
      // ...your existing params
      client_reference_id: trackingIdFromTheClick,
    })
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match the payment to the post">
    On the completed payment (via a Stripe webhook), read `client_reference_id`, find the click it belongs to, and find the post that click came from. The revenue now belongs to that post.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The honest-attribution rule

If a payment arrives with no tracking id — or one that doesn't resolve to a click — **don't guess.** Leave it unattributed rather than crediting a random post. Attribution is only useful if you can trust it, and a fabricated match is worse than an honest "we don't know."

The same applies to **refunds and chargebacks**: when Stripe reverses a payment, subtract it back out of the post's earnings, so a post's revenue always reflects money you actually kept.

## How seenpaid does it for you

You don't wire any of this by hand:

- **Tracked links** are minted automatically when you publish a post.
- **The pixel** (one `<script>` tag) auto-attaches the tracking id to Stripe Payment Links, Buy Buttons, and Checkout — see [Track your sales](/guides/track-your-sales).
- **Matching** runs off your connected Stripe via [Stripe Connect](/guides/connect-stripe), including recurring subscriptions and refund reversals.
- **[Attribution health](/how-attribution-works)** shows what share of your sales were traced to a post, so gaps never hide.
