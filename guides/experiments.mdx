**Experiments** let you test caption wording with real posts. You give one idea; seenpaid writes a few variants; each goes out as a real post; the results decide the winner.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the test">
    Give an **idea**, a **platform**, and how many **variants** you want (2 to 8, default 3). seenpaid drafts them. Nothing is live yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch it">
    Every variant publishes as its own post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the numbers decide">
    The dashboard shows each variant with its results and marks the leader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reuse the winner">
    Take the winning caption into the composer and scale it: repost it, recycle it, or use it as the template for the next batch. Experiments live at **seenpaid.com/experiments** and in your agent's tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What decides the winner

- With **revenue attribution on**, each variant carries its own tracked link and the winner is the variant that **earned the most**. Ties go to clicks.
- With attribution **off**, variants publish as plain posts with no tracked links, so there are no clicks or sales to compare and no winner can be called. Turn attribution on before launching a test. See [Turn on revenue attribution](/guides/turn-on-revenue-attribution).

<Info>
  Because every variant is its own post with its own link, attribution is clean: a sale can only belong to the variant whose link the buyer clicked.
</Info>

## Driving it from an agent

- [`create_experiment`](/agents/tools-reference#create_experiment): idea + platform + variant count.
- [`launch_experiment`](/agents/tools-reference#launch_experiment): publish every variant.
- [`list_experiments`](/agents/tools-reference#list_experiments) / [`get_experiment`](/agents/tools-reference#get_experiment): status and per-variant results.
- [`get_experiment_winner`](/agents/tools-reference#get_experiment_winner): the winning caption.

> *"A/B test three hooks for my launch post on X, launch them, and tell me which one wins."*
