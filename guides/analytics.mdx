Revenue tells you *what earned*. The breakdowns on **Results** (under **More breakdowns**) tell you *where it came from*. They need [revenue attribution](/guides/turn-on-revenue-attribution) on, because they're built from tracked clicks.

## The breakdowns

Every dimension is ranked by clicks with the attributed revenue for each row beside it.

## Direct, Unknown, None

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Direct** | No referrer: typed URL, in-app browser, or stripped referrer. Still a real, attributed click. |
| **Unknown** | That dimension couldn't be resolved: no geolocation, or an unrecognised device, OS or browser. |
| **None** | The link carried no UTM tag for that field. |

A large Direct share is normal for social traffic.

## The funnel and goals

```
clicks → leads → sales
```

**Leads** are mid-funnel conversions you report from your own site with `window.seenpaidTrackLead(email, kind)`; **goals** are any named conversion you fire with `window.seenpaidTrackGoal(name)`. Both are optional. Lots of clicks and few sales points at the landing page or offer; clicks but no leads points at the step before checkout. Results also shows a live count of visitors active in the last five minutes.

## The window

Every breakdown respects the window you pick (7, 30 or 90 days). For day-by-day trends ask an agent for [`get_analytics_timeseries`](/agents/tools-reference#get_analytics_timeseries); for a single dimension, [`get_analytics_breakdown`](/agents/tools-reference#get_analytics_breakdown).
