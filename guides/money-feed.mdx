**Live activity** on Results is the newest-first stream of what's happening: every tracked click, matched sale and refund, tagged with the platform and country it came from. It refreshes itself, and it needs [revenue attribution](/guides/turn-on-revenue-attribution) on.

## What's in the stream

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Clicks" icon="hand-pointer">
    A tap on a tracked link.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sales" icon="dollar-sign">
    A matched payment, with the amount and the post it belongs to.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Refunds" icon="rotate-left">
    A reversal, subtracted from the post's earnings.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

A sale appears the moment its webhook is matched to a click, usually within seconds of the payment.

## Alerts

seenpaid raises **notifications** (with an unread count) for sales that landed, posts that failed to publish, and revenue milestones. Your agent reads them with [`list_notifications`](/agents/tools-reference#list_notifications).

## Emails

- **Welcome**, from Justinas, the founder. Replying reaches him.
- **Your post didn't publish**, naming the platform; **Reconnect**, when an account needs re-authorizing; and **LinkedIn expires soon**, about a week before a LinkedIn grant's 60-day expiry.
- **First sale**: sent once per post, the first time a payment is matched to it. Never once per sale. Requires attribution on.
- Two short trial emails (day 2 and day 4) and a **weekly digest**.
- Password reset and support confirmations.

There is no "your card is charged tomorrow" reminder; the trial end date is shown under **Settings → Billing**.
