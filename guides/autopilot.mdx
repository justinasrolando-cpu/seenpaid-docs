---
title: "Autopilot"
description: "Let seenpaid draft posts on a cadence you set, in a tone you choose. Nothing publishes without your approval."
---

**Autopilot** keeps your pipeline full by generating post drafts on a schedule. You set the topic, tone, cadence and platforms; seenpaid writes the drafts; you approve the ones you like and schedule them. It never publishes on its own.

## When to use it

- You want to post consistently but keep running out of ideas.
- You have a clear topic and would rather edit and approve than start from a blank page.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Agent and configure Autopilot">
    In the left rail, click **Agent**. Set:
    - **Cadence**: a new batch every 1 to 30 days.
    - **Batch size**: 1 to 10 drafts per batch.
    - **Platforms**: which channels to write for.
    - **Topic**: what to write about.
    - **Tone**: default, direct, friendly, bold, analytical or playful, and whether to use emoji.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let a batch generate">
    Drafts land in a queue. You can also generate a batch now instead of waiting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or dismiss">
    Approve the drafts you like; dismiss the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the keepers">
    Approving hands you the caption and intended platform. Schedule it like any other post.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Autopilot **never publishes by itself.** Approving a draft does not send it; you (or an agent acting on your instruction) still put it on the calendar.
</Warning>

<Note>
  When revenue attribution is on and you have earning posts, drafts are written in the voice of your top earners. With attribution off, they follow your topic and tone settings.
</Note>

## Driving it from an agent

- [`configure_autopilot`](/agents/tools-reference#configure_autopilot): on/off, cadence, batch size, platforms, topic, tone.
- [`get_autopilot_status`](/agents/tools-reference#get_autopilot_status): is it on, and how many drafts are waiting.
- [`generate_suggestions`](/agents/tools-reference#generate_suggestions): run a batch now.
- [`list_suggestions`](/agents/tools-reference#list_suggestions) / [`approve_suggestion`](/agents/tools-reference#approve_suggestion) / [`dismiss_suggestion`](/agents/tools-reference#dismiss_suggestion): work the queue.

> *"Turn on autopilot: 3 drafts every 4 days about my product, for X and LinkedIn, friendly tone, no emoji. Then show me the queue."*

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I asked to generate now and nothing happened">
    Autopilot has to be configured and switched on first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Approving a draft didn't post it">
    By design. Approval returns the caption for you to schedule.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
