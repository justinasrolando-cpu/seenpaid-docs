Once posts are published and Stripe is connected, **Revenue** is where the payoff shows up — your content, ranked by the money it actually made. This is the number no other scheduler shows you.

## Earnings per post

Every published post carries its attributed revenue: how many tracked clicks it drove, how many turned into sales, and the total dollars matched to it. Click into a post to see its full attribution.

## Top earners

seenpaid ranks your posts by revenue so your best-performing content rises to the top on its own. Use it to answer the only question that matters: **what should I post more of?** When you find a winner, don't let it die in the feed — [Recycle](/guides/recycle) it so it keeps earning.

## Dead posts

The counterpart to top earners, and often the more useful list: posts that **got clicks but earned nothing**. This is content that looked like it worked — it pulled reach and taps — but produced no tracked sales. It tells you what to *stop* making. You can filter by a minimum click count so you're only looking at posts that got a real audience.

<Info>
  Dead posts are the fastest way to cut wasted effort. A post with lots of clicks and zero revenue is a signal about the offer or the landing page, not just the caption — worth investigating before you make more like it.
</Info>

## Revenue by platform, and channel ROI

See which network drives **sales**, not just engagement. A post can rack up likes on one platform and revenue on another — this view makes the difference obvious. Channel ROI goes further and ranks platforms by **revenue per post** and **revenue per click**, the honest measures of whether a channel is worth your time, instead of follower counts.

## Attribution health — trust the number first

Before reading a low revenue figure as bad news, check **Attribution health**. It shows what share of your real Stripe revenue seenpaid could trace back to a post. A low match rate means your tracking install has a gap — usually the [pixel](/guides/track-your-sales) isn't on your checkout page — **not** that your posts failed. Fix the gap and revenue you'd already earned starts showing against the right posts.

## Before you have data

Attribution needs a published tracked post and a connected Stripe account. Until your first sale matches, the revenue views show clearly-marked **sample/preview states** so you can see what's coming. As soon as real sales match, they populate with your own numbers.

<Note>
  seenpaid only ever shows real, matched revenue. Preview states are labelled as samples and are never mixed into your actual totals — no fabricated credit, no inflated dashboard.
</Note>

## A weekly loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read top earners and dead posts">
    Double down on what earned; stop making what didn't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check channel ROI">
    Spend your effort on the platform with the best revenue per post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recycle a winner, test a new hook">
    Keep a proven post in rotation while you [experiment](/guides/experiments) on the next idea.
  </Step>
</Steps>
