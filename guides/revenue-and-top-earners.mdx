Once posts are published and Stripe is connected, the **Revenue** area is where the payoff shows up — your content, ranked by the money it actually made.

## Earnings per post

Every published post carries its attributed revenue: how many tracked clicks it drove, how many turned into sales, and the total dollars matched to it. This is the number no other scheduler shows you.

## Top earners

seenpaid ranks your posts by revenue so your best-performing content rises to the top on its own. Use it to answer the only question that matters: **what should I post more of?**

<Tip>
  Found a top earner? Don't let it die in the feed — [Recycle](/guides/recycle) re-queues your proven winners so they keep earning.
</Tip>

## Revenue by platform

See which network drives sales, not just engagement. A post can rack up likes on one platform and revenue on another — this view makes the difference obvious, so you can spend your time where the money is.

## Before you have data

Attribution needs a published tracked post and a connected Stripe account. Until your first post goes out, the revenue views show sample/preview states so you can see what's coming. As soon as real sales match, they populate with your own numbers.

<Note>
  seenpaid only ever shows real, matched revenue. Preview states are clearly marked as samples and are never mixed into your actual totals.
</Note>
