With [revenue attribution](/guides/turn-on-revenue-attribution) on and a revenue source connected, **Results** adds the money side to every post.

## What Results shows

- **Totals** for the window (7, 30 or 90 days): revenue, sales, conversion (share of clicks that became sales) and revenue per click, each with the change vs the previous period.
- **By post**: every post that went out, ranked by what it earned, with clicks and sales beside it. Filter by channel with the tabs.
- **Live activity**: the newest-first stream of clicks, sales and refunds. See [Money feed & alerts](/guides/money-feed).
- **More breakdowns**: campaigns, devices, referrers, the clicks → leads → sales funnel and named goals. See [Analytics](/guides/analytics).
- **Sales traced to a post**: of every payment seenpaid has seen, how many it could match to a post. This is the honesty check. A low share means the tracking install has a gap (usually the [pixel](/guides/track-your-sales) is missing from the checkout page), not that your posts failed.
- **Export CSV** and a one-click **email digest** of the current view.

## Top earners and dead posts

The by-post table ranked by revenue is your top-earners list. The counterpart, posts that got clicks but earned nothing, is available to your agent as [`get_dead_posts`](/agents/tools-reference#get_dead_posts); it's the list that tells you what to stop making. Channel ROI (revenue per post and per click, per platform) is [`get_channel_roi`](/agents/tools-reference#get_channel_roi).

## Before you have data

Until your first sale matches, the money views show a setup checklist rather than numbers. seenpaid only ever shows real, matched revenue; nothing is estimated.

## A weekly loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the ranking">
    Double down on what earned; stop making what didn't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the traced share">
    If it's low, fix the pixel before reading a low revenue figure as bad news.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recycle a winner, test a new hook">
    [Recycle](/guides/recycle) a proven post while you [experiment](/guides/experiments) on the next idea.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  An agent reads the whole picture with [`summarize_performance`](/agents/tools-reference#summarize_performance): *"Give me a briefing on the last 7 days and tell me what to do next."*
</Tip>
