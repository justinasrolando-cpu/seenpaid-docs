---
title: "Track your sales"
description: "Install the seenpaid pixel so Stripe sales get matched to the post that drove them."
---

seenpaid matches a sale back to a post when the sale carries the post's **tracking id**. The pixel does that for you — paste one tag and it attaches the id to your Stripe checkout automatically.

<Note>
  You only need this if you sell on **your own site** (a landing page, a store). Selling straight from a Stripe Payment Link in your bio? The tracked link already carries the id — you're done.
</Note>

## Install the pixel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your tag">
    In the dashboard, go to **Settings → Install tracking on your store** and copy the tag. It looks like this:

    ```html
    <script src="https://api.seenpaid.com/pixel.js"></script>
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in your site's <head>">
    Add it to every page where someone can buy — ideally site-wide, in your `<head>`. If you use a site builder (Framer, Webflow, Carrd, Shopify), paste it into the "custom head code" / "embed" field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="That's it">
    The pixel automatically attaches the tracking id to **Stripe Payment Links, Buy Buttons, and Checkout links**. Nothing else to wire.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two special cases

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My checkout is on a custom domain">
    If your Payment Link runs on your own domain (not `buy.stripe.com`), the pixel can't recognize it by URL. Tag the button or link with `data-seenpaid` and it'll be handled:

    ```html
    <a href="https://pay.yourbrand.com/abc" data-seenpaid>Buy now</a>
    ```
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I build my own Checkout Session server-side">
    Read the id on the client and pass it through as one field when you create the session:

    ```js
    // client: send window.seenpaidTrackingId to your backend
    const session = await stripe.checkout.sessions.create({
      // ...your existing params
      client_reference_id: trackingIdFromTheClient,
    })
    ```

    `client_reference_id` is the only field seenpaid needs — everything else is automatic.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Check it's working

Once Stripe is connected and a real sale comes through a tracked link, open **Revenue → Attribution health**. It shows what share of your sales were traced to a post.

<Warning>
  Seeing sales but a **low match rate**? That almost always means the pixel isn't on your checkout page yet — double-check the tag is in the `<head>` of the page where people actually pay.
</Warning>
