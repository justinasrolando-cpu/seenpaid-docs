---
title: "Recycle top earners"
description: "Turn your proven money-makers into a schedule that keeps re-posting them."
---

Your best post already proved it converts. **Recycle** puts that proof to work — automatically re-queuing your top-earning posts so your winning content keeps earning instead of scrolling out of the feed forever.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="seenpaid ranks your earners">
    Using real attributed revenue, seenpaid knows which posts made the most money over the last 30 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Auto-recycle">
    In **Recycle**, enable auto-recycle. seenpaid schedules your top earners to go out again at sensible times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They earn again">
    Re-posted winners get fresh tracked links, so the new round of clicks and sales is attributed just like the first — you can see the compounding effect in Revenue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Recycling is most powerful once you have a few weeks of attributed data — the more seenpaid knows about what pays, the better its picks.
</Tip>
