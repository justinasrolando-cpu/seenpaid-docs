---
title: "Recycle"
description: "Re-post content that already worked, either once or on a standing rule."
---

Good posts scroll out of the feed in a day. **Recycle** brings them back: repost a specific post as a brand-new post, or set a standing rule that re-queues your proven earners on a cadence.

## One-off: repost a post

Open any published post and repost it. It goes out as a new post, optionally with a new caption, a new time or different channels. The original is untouched. With attribution on, the copy gets fresh tracking so its clicks and sales are counted on their own.

## Standing rule: auto-recycle

Auto-recycle re-posts posts that have **earned at least a threshold you choose**, no more often than a **cadence** you choose (1 to 90 days). It reads attributed revenue, so it needs [revenue attribution](/guides/turn-on-revenue-attribution) on and at least one post with a matched sale; with no earning posts, nothing qualifies and nothing is re-posted.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the threshold">
    The minimum revenue a post must have earned to qualify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the cadence">
    The minimum days between re-posts, so a winner doesn't spam the same audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Qualifying posts are re-queued automatically, each as a new post with fresh tracking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Driving it from an agent

- [`repost`](/agents/tools-reference#repost): re-run a specific post now or later, optionally with a new caption or channels.
- [`get_recycle_status`](/agents/tools-reference#get_recycle_status): the current rule and which posts qualify.
- [`configure_recycle`](/agents/tools-reference#configure_recycle): set the threshold and cadence.

> *"Repost my top earner for next Tuesday."*
> *"Auto-recycle any post that earned over $100, at most once every 14 days."*
