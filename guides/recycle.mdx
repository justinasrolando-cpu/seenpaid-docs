---
title: "Recycle top earners"
description: "Turn your proven money-makers into a schedule that keeps re-posting them — with fresh tracking each time."
---

Your best post already proved it converts. **Recycle** puts that proof to work — automatically re-queuing your top-earning posts so your winning content keeps earning instead of scrolling out of the feed forever. Each re-post gets a fresh tracked link, so the new round of clicks and sales is attributed just like the first.

## When to use it

- You have a handful of posts that clearly earned, and re-writing them from scratch is wasted effort.
- You want a baseline of proven content going out on a cadence, so slow weeks still produce revenue.
- You've found an [experiment](/guides/experiments) winner and want it to keep working.

Recycle is most powerful once you have a few weeks of attributed data — the more seenpaid knows about what pays, the better its picks.

## Two ways to recycle

### One-off: repost a specific winner

Find a top earner and run it again as a brand-new post with fresh tracking. The original is left untouched. This is the manual, "do it now" version — great right after you spot a winner in [Revenue](/guides/revenue-and-top-earners).

### Standing rule: auto-recycle

Set a rule and let it run. seenpaid re-posts anything that earned at least a threshold you choose, no more often than a cadence you choose.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the threshold">
    Choose the **minimum revenue** a post must have earned to qualify (e.g. $100). Only real winners get recycled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the cadence">
    Choose the **minimum days** between re-posts of the same content, so a winner doesn't spam the same audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    seenpaid schedules qualifying posts to go out again, each with a fresh tracked link. You watch the compounding effect in Revenue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Every recycled post is a **new** post with its own tracked link — so its clicks and sales are attributed cleanly, and you can see a winner's second and third runs earn on their own.
</Info>

## Reading the results

Because each run is tracked separately, Revenue shows you whether a recycled winner keeps converting or fades — so you can retire a post that's run its course and promote a fresher one. Recycling isn't "post the same thing forever"; it's "keep proven content in rotation while it still pays."

## Driving it from an agent

- [`get_recycle_status`](/agents/tools-reference#get_recycle_status) — the current rule and which posts qualify.
- [`configure_recycle`](/agents/tools-reference#configure_recycle) — set the threshold and cadence.
- [`repost`](/agents/tools-reference#repost) — re-run a specific post now, optionally on new platforms or with a new caption.

> *"Repost my top earner for next Tuesday."*
> *"Auto-recycle any post that earned over $100, at most once every 14 days."*
