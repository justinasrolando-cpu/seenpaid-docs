---
title: "Connect Stripe"
description: "Wire up Stripe so seenpaid can match sales to the posts that drove them."
---

Connecting Stripe is the step that turns seenpaid from a scheduler into an attribution engine. It's a standard Stripe Connect authorization — read-only where it matters, and revocable anytime.

## Connect your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections → Stripe">
    In the dashboard, go to **Connections** and find the **Stripe** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Stripe">
    You'll be sent to Stripe to authorize seenpaid via Stripe Connect. Approve, and you're returned to the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it's live">
    Stripe shows as connected. From now on, completed payments are checked against your tracked clicks and attributed to the source post.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  seenpaid reads payment events to attribute revenue. It does not move money, create charges, or change your Stripe settings.
</Check>

## What gets attributed

Once connected, seenpaid listens for the Stripe events that represent real revenue:

- Completed Checkout Sessions and Payment Links
- Direct charges and payments created via the Stripe API
- Recurring subscription invoices
- Refunds and chargebacks — reversed back out of a post's earnings

See [How attribution works](/how-attribution-works) for the full matching chain.

## Multiple Stripe accounts

Running more than one business? seenpaid supports connecting multiple Stripe accounts and keeps each one's revenue scoped to its own workspace, so numbers never bleed across brands.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sales aren't showing up against posts">
    Two things must be true: Stripe is connected, and the buyer clicked a **seenpaid tracked link** on a published post. A sale from a visitor who never touched a tracked link can't be attributed to a post.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to disconnect">
    You can revoke seenpaid's access from your Stripe dashboard under **Settings → Connected apps** at any time, or from Connections in seenpaid.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
