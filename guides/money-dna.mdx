title: "Money DNA"
description: "The patterns behind your earning posts — best platform, ideal caption length, whether links and media help you, and your best day."
---

**Money DNA** answers a question you can't eyeball: *why* do your posts make money? It looks only at posts that actually earned tracked revenue, finds what they have in common, and turns that into a handful of plain-language rules for your account specifically — not generic best-practice.

## When to use it

- You've published for a few weeks and want to know what's working **for you**, not for social media in general.
- You're about to plan the next month of content and want to lean into your own winners.
- You want a sanity check before believing a "best time to post" article — Money DNA uses your real revenue instead.

## What it tells you

Once you have enough earning posts, Money DNA surfaces up to five insights, each a card with a headline metric and a one-line explanation:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your money platform" icon="crown">
    The single platform that has driven the most attributed revenue. Post there more.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ideal length" icon="ruler">
    Whether your short (under 120 chars), medium (120–500), or long (500+) posts earn the most on average.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Do links pay off?" icon="link">
    Whether posts with a tracked link out-earn those without — with the percentage lift for *your* account. Sometimes the answer is "lead with value, drop the link in a reply."
  </Card>
  <Card title="Does media sell?" icon="image">
    Whether posts with an image or video earn more than plain text for you. Sometimes text wins — so you don't over-produce.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your best day" icon="calendar-day">
    The weekday your posts have earned the most. Schedule your big drops then.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Why it stays quiet at first

Money DNA needs at least **3 earning posts** before it will report a pattern, and each individual insight needs at least two posts on each side of a comparison to be meaningful. Below that, any "pattern" is just noise — so seenpaid tells you honestly how many earning posts you have so far rather than inventing precision.

<Info>
  DNA is computed from **matched revenue**, so it only sees a post once a real Stripe sale has been attributed to it. Publishing more tracked posts and connecting Stripe is what feeds it.
</Info>

## How to act on it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lean into your money platform">
    If one platform drives most of your revenue, weight your effort there rather than spreading thin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match the format that pays">
    Write to your ideal length, and include (or skip) links and media based on what actually earns for you — not a generic rule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Time your big posts">
    Schedule launches and drops on your best day. The [next-slot suggestion](/guides/publish-and-schedule) uses this same signal automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>
