---
title: "Bio page"
description: "A hosted link-in-bio page that keeps Instagram and TikTok attributable: one link in your profile, tracked per post."
---

Instagram and TikTok don't make caption links clickable, so a tracked link in a caption there is dead text. The **bio page** is a simple hosted page you put in your profile's link field, where each link is a tracked seenpaid link. It's part of [revenue attribution](/guides/turn-on-revenue-attribution); with attribution off you don't need it.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your page">
    In **Settings → Bio page**, pick a **handle** (up to 30 characters), a title and optionally an avatar. Your page lives at `seenpaid.com/bio/<handle>`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the URL into your profile">
    Paste it into the link-in-bio field on Instagram and TikTok.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Links get tracked per post">
    Each link on the page is a `go.seenpaid.com/r/<id>` tracked link tied to a post and platform. A tap is logged against that post, and a sale that follows is attributed to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The public page is unauthenticated and shows a friendly "no such page" for an unknown handle. An agent can add a link with [`create_tracked_link`](/agents/tools-reference#create_tracked_link).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Clicks from Instagram or TikTok aren't attributed">
    Make sure the link people tap is the one on your bio page, not a raw URL pasted in a caption.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
