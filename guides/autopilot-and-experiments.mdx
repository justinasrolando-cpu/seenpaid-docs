Beyond one-off posting and recycling, seenpaid has two standing automations that keep working between the times you (or your agent) check in: **autopilot** drafts posts on a schedule, and **experiments** A/B test captions and let real revenue pick the winner.

Both are available in the dashboard and over [MCP](/agents/overview), so an agent can set a policy once and then just monitor it.

## Autopilot

Autopilot generates fresh post drafts on a cadence you set — so you're never staring at a blank composer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure it">
    Set a **cadence** (a new batch every N days), a **batch size** (how many drafts per batch), the **platforms** to write for, and a **topic** — e.g. "indie SaaS growth lessons".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the suggestions">
    Each batch lands as drafts waiting for a decision. Approve the ones you like, dismiss the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the keepers">
    Approving a draft hands you its caption — you then schedule it like any other post, with a tracked link attached automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Autopilot **never publishes on its own.** It only ever produces drafts; approval and scheduling are always a deliberate step. Nothing goes out without a human — or an agent acting on your instruction — putting it on the calendar.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Driving this from an agent? The tools are `configure_autopilot`, `get_autopilot_status`, `generate_suggestions`, `list_suggestions`, `approve_suggestion`, and `dismiss_suggestion`. See the [tools reference](/agents/tools-reference#automation).
</Note>

## Experiments — A/B testing by revenue

Most "A/B testing" for social measures likes. seenpaid measures **money**. You give one idea and a platform; seenpaid writes a few caption variants; each goes out as a real post with its **own tracked link**, so revenue can be attributed to each variant independently. The winner is the one that earned — not the one that got the most engagement.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the test">
    Give an idea and a platform, and choose how many variants (2–4). seenpaid drafts the variants. Nothing is live yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch it">
    Publish every variant. Each carries a distinct tracked link, so clicks and sales land on the right variant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the winner">
    As sales come in, seenpaid ranks the variants by revenue. Reuse or scale up the winning caption.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Pair experiments with [Recycle](/guides/recycle): once an experiment names a revenue winner, recycle keeps that proven caption earning instead of letting it scroll out of the feed.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Agent tools: `create_experiment`, `launch_experiment`, `list_experiments`, `get_experiment`, and `get_experiment_winner`.
</Note>
