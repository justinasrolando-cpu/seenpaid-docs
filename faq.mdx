## The basics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is seenpaid, in one line?">
    A social media scheduler with a built-in AI agent: write once, publish or schedule to 21 platforms, read engagement back, and optionally see which posts made money.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to use revenue attribution?">
    No. It is an optional add-on and it is **off** for new workspaces. With it off, seenpaid is a complete scheduler: composer, calendar, bulk upload, autopilot, engagement and the agent all work. Turn it on any time under **Settings → Attribution**. See [Turn on revenue attribution](/guides/turn-on-revenue-attribution).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to be technical to use it?">
    No. The [getting-started journey](/start/create-your-account) is click-by-click. The only "code" anywhere is one optional script tag on your own site, and only if you turn on attribution and sell there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a demo?">
    Not a public one. The 7-day trial is the full product; see [Create your account](/start/create-your-account).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Posting and links

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens to the links in my posts?">
    With attribution **off** (the default), links go out exactly as you wrote them. Nothing is rewritten, shortened or tagged. With attribution **on**, each link is replaced by a `go.seenpaid.com/r/<id>` tracked link, or, if you choose own-URL mode, your link stays yours with a `cp_tid` parameter appended. You can flip the switch in **Settings → Attribution**; the change applies from your next publish.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I tailor the caption per network?">
    Yes. Every channel inherits the main caption until you edit it, and each has its own counter against that platform's real limit. You can also translate the caption per account. See [Publish & schedule](/guides/publish-and-schedule).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit a post after scheduling it?">
    Yes, until it publishes: caption, time, or both. Once it's out the platforms already have it, so repost a new version instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why didn't my TikTok post go out automatically?">
    seenpaid delivers the video to your TikTok **drafts (inbox)**. Open TikTok, find the draft, write the caption there and tap Post. The caption you wrote in seenpaid is not sent, because TikTok's draft endpoint accepts only the video. Video only, 64MB or under.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Platforms

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which platforms can I connect?">
    21: X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads, Bluesky, Pinterest, Mastodon, Discord, Telegram, Tumblr, Slack, WordPress, Ghost, Dev.to, Hashnode, Medium, Lemmy, Nostr and a webhook. Three carry conditions today: Instagram and Facebook connect only for accounts added to seenpaid's Meta app (Meta App Review has not passed), Pinterest connects but publishing currently fails on Pinterest's side (Trial access), and X needs your own developer keys. Full table: [Supported platforms](/guides/platforms).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I post to X for free?">
    X charges for API posting on your own developer account; that's X's pricing, not seenpaid's. You paste your own X app keys and posting depends on credits on that X account. Every other platform posts at no extra cost.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which networks report likes, comments and views back?">
    13 of the 21: Bluesky, Mastodon, X, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, Tumblr, Discord, Dev.to, Hashnode, WordPress, Lemmy and Nostr, each with whatever its API exposes (Discord is likes only, WordPress comments only, and so on). Threads, LinkedIn and TikTok need permissions seenpaid doesn't currently hold; Slack, Telegram, Medium, Ghost and Webhook expose nothing to read. A missing number means "not reported", never zero. Table: [Engagement metrics](/guides/engagement).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often is engagement refreshed?">
    Every 4 hours for a post's first 48 hours, then daily until day 30. X is read once a day (X bills per read).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reconnecting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How often do I have to reconnect my accounts?">
    Rarely. Facebook and Instagram Page tokens don't expire, so those stay connected. Threads and Bluesky renew themselves (Bluesky falls back to your app password if its session lapses). **LinkedIn** is the exception: LinkedIn's standard grant lasts 60 days with no refresh, so it needs a reconnect every 60 days; seenpaid emails you about a week before with a reconnect link. Other networks refresh their own tokens in the background.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if a token expires right when a post goes out?">
    The publish job refreshes the token once and retries. Only if the platform refuses the refresh does the account flip to **needs reconnecting**, with an email and a Reconnect button under Channels. A platform outage, rate limit or timeout never logs you out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does reconnecting duplicate my account?">
    No. Reconnecting updates the existing account in place; your history and scheduled posts stay attached.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes, 7 days. You pick a plan and save a card to start it; you're charged $0 today and the first charge is on day 8, only if you haven't cancelled. One free trial per customer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a permanent free plan?">
    No. After the trial, a plan keeps the account running. Connecting accounts and publishing need an active trial or subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do the plans include?">
    Every plan has every feature. They differ only in connected social accounts: **Solo** 5 ($19.99/mo), **Multi-Brand** 15 ($39.99/mo), **Omni** unlimited ($69.99/mo). Yearly is ten months' price. Revenue-source connections aren't capped on any plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    **Settings → Billing → Cancel**, any time. No call, no email. You keep access until the end of the period you're in. Cancelling during the trial means you're never charged.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a lifetime deal?">
    Not for sale. The only way to a lifetime account is the [referral ladder](/guides/referrals) at 25 converted referrals.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Revenue attribution (optional)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does seenpaid know which post made a sale?">
    A tracked link carries an id (`cp_tid`) from the click, through your site, into your payment provider (as `client_reference_id` on Stripe). When the payment webhook fires, seenpaid matches that id back to the click and the post. Full walkthrough: [How attribution works](/how-attribution-works).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which payment providers work?">
    Stripe (via Stripe Connect), plus Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Whop, Shopify, Paddle, Polar and Dodo Payments by webhook. See [Connect a revenue source](/guides/connect-stripe).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if a sale can't be traced to a post?">
    It's recorded as revenue but left **unattributed**, never credited to a random post. **Sales traced to a post** on Results shows what share was traceable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it handle subscriptions and refunds?">
    Yes. Recurring subscription invoices are attributed, and refunds and chargebacks are subtracted back out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I turn attribution off later?">
    Posts go back to publishing with untouched links, Results shows engagement only, and the MCP money tools reply that attribution is off. Nothing is deleted; your clicks and sales come back when you turn it on again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy & AI agents

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does seenpaid use third-party cookies or track people across sites?">
    No. With attribution off, nothing is tracked at all. With it on, the `cp_tid` cookie is first-party on your own site; there's no third-party cookie and no cross-site tracking network, and the pixel does nothing on a visit that didn't come from a tracked link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can an AI agent run my account?">
    Yes. seenpaid ships an MCP server with 50 tools: an agent (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Hermes, or your own) can schedule, validate, bulk-plan, generate media and read results with an API key. Start with [your first agent action](/agents/quickstart). The revenue tools work once attribution is on; the rest work regardless.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can an agent see other people's data?">
    Never. Every API key is scoped to one workspace, and the server is built fresh per request bound to that workspace. There is no cross-account access.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
