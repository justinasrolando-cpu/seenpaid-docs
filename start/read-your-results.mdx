**Results** is where you see how posts did once they're out.

## Engagement per post

For every published post, Results shows **views, likes, comments and shares**, as reported by each platform. Use the channel tabs to see all networks merged or one at a time.

Two rules worth knowing:

- **13 of the 21 platforms report something.** Bluesky, Mastodon, X, Facebook, Instagram, Lemmy, Dev.to, Hashnode, WordPress, Nostr, Discord, Tumblr and Pinterest. Threads, LinkedIn and TikTok need permissions seenpaid doesn't currently hold, and Slack, Telegram, Medium, Ghost and Webhook expose nothing to read.
- **A missing number means "not reported", never zero.** If a platform doesn't give us a count, the cell shows a dash. seenpaid never fills a gap with a 0.

Counts are read on a schedule: every four hours for a post's first two days, then daily until day 30. The full per-platform table is in [Engagement metrics](/guides/engagement).

## With revenue attribution on

If you turned on [revenue attribution](/guides/turn-on-revenue-attribution), Results adds the money side: clicks, sales and revenue per post, top earners, dead posts (clicks but no sales), revenue by platform, a live money feed, and an attribution-health figure that says what share of your payments could be traced to a post. Those views are covered in the [Optional: revenue attribution](/guides/revenue-and-top-earners) section.

If it's off, Results shows engagement only, plus one quiet card offering to turn attribution on. Nothing else is hidden or gated.
