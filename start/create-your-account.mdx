Welcome. This is the first stop on a six-step journey that takes you from a brand-new account to your first sale matched to the exact post that drove it. You don't need to be technical, and you don't need to finish it all in one sitting — each step tells you what to click, what you'll see, and what "done" looks like.

<Info>
  **The whole journey, in order:** create your account → connect a channel → publish your first post → install the tracking pixel → connect Stripe → read your money dashboard. This page is step 1.
</Info>

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-up page">
    Open [seenpaid.com/signup](https://seenpaid.com/signup) and create your account with your email. This takes under a minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land in your dashboard">
    After signing up you're dropped straight into the dashboard. The left rail is your home base — Posts, Revenue, Channels, Agent, and Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your free trial">
    To connect a channel or publish, start your **7-day free trial**: open **Settings → Billing**, pick a plan, and enter your card. **You are not charged today** — the card is saved so your account keeps working when the trial ends. Cancel any time in those 7 days and you won't be billed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  seenpaid has **no permanent free tier**. The trial is what unlocks connecting accounts and publishing — it's a full-featured 7 days, not a limited demo. Starting it is what turns the dashboard from a preview into a working account.
</Note>

## What the trial gives you

For 7 days you get the whole product. What differs between plans is scale, not features:

| Plan | Social accounts | Stripe accounts (revenue sources) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | 15 | 1 |
| Growth | 50 | 3 |
| Pro | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Lifetime | Unlimited | Unlimited |

<Info>
  A **Stripe account** is a revenue source — the connection that lets seenpaid see your sales and match them to posts. Most solo founders need one; agencies running several businesses connect several. See [Connect Stripe](/start/connect-stripe) later in this journey.
</Info>

## What "done" looks like

You're ready for the next step when:

- You can see your dashboard with the left rail (Posts, Revenue, Channels, Agent, Settings).
- Your trial is active — **Settings → Billing** shows a plan and a trial end date, not a "start your trial" prompt.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I signed up but everything asks me to 'start my trial'">
    That's expected. Connecting a channel and publishing are gated behind an active trial or subscription. Open **Settings → Billing**, choose a plan, and enter your card — you won't be charged for 7 days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will I be charged today?">
    No. The card is saved at sign-up to your trial; the first charge is at the end of the 7 days, and only if you haven't cancelled. There's no charge on day one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I didn't get a confirmation email">
    Check spam first. If it still hasn't arrived, try signing in at [seenpaid.com/login](https://seenpaid.com/login) — your account may already be active.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
