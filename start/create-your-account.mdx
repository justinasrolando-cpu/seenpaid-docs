This is the first of four short steps: create your account, connect a channel, write your first post, read the results. You don't need to be technical, and each step says what "done" looks like.

## Sign up and start the trial

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    Open [seenpaid.com/signup](https://seenpaid.com/signup) and sign up with your email. You'll get a welcome email from Justinas, the founder; replying to it reaches him directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan and save a card">
    Setup opens on the plan picker. Choose a plan and enter a card to start your **7-day free trial**. **You are charged $0 today.** The first charge is on day 8, and only if you haven't cancelled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how you'll use seenpaid">
    The next screen asks **How will you use seenpaid?**

    - **Scheduling** (default): posts go out with your links exactly as written; Results shows engagement.
    - **Scheduling + revenue**: turns on [revenue attribution](/guides/turn-on-revenue-attribution), which adds tracked links and matches sales to posts.

    This is just a switch. Change it any time in **Settings → Attribution**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a channel and write a post">
    Setup walks you straight into the next two steps, covered on the following pages. You can also skip ahead and do them from the dashboard later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  seenpaid has **no permanent free tier**. Connecting accounts and publishing need an active trial or subscription. The trial is the full product for 7 days, not a limited version.
</Note>

## The plans

Every plan includes every feature. The only difference is how many social accounts you can connect.

| Plan | Connected accounts | Monthly | Yearly |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Solo | 5 | $19.99 | $199.99 |
| Multi-Brand | 15 | $39.99 | $399.99 |
| Omni | Unlimited | $69.99 | $699.99 |

Yearly is ten months' price, so two months are free. Revenue-source connections (Stripe and the others) are not capped on any plan.

<Info>
  One free trial per customer. If you cancel and later come back, you pay from day one rather than starting a second trial.
</Info>

## What "done" looks like

- You're in the dashboard. The left rail shows **New post**, **Bulk upload**, **Calendar**, **All posts**, **Results**, **Channels**, **Agent**, **Settings** and **Billing**.
- **Settings → Billing** shows your plan and a trial end date.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Everything asks me to start my trial">
    Connecting a channel and publishing are gated behind an active trial or subscription. Open **Settings → Billing**, choose a plan and enter a card. You won't be charged for 7 days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will I be charged today?">
    No. The card is saved to the trial; the first charge is on day 8, and only if you haven't cancelled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I didn't get the welcome email">
    Check spam first. Then try signing in at [seenpaid.com/login](https://seenpaid.com/login); your account is already active either way.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
