You're set up. Now for the payoff: reading the dashboard that no other scheduler shows you — your content ranked by the money it actually made. This page is a tour of what each view means and how to act on it.

<Info>
  Everything here needs two things live: at least one **published tracked post** and a **connected Stripe account**. Before your first matched sale, these views show clearly-marked sample states so you can see what's coming. Real numbers replace them the moment a sale matches.
</Info>

## Revenue — earnings per post

The **Revenue** area is home base for the money side. Every published post carries its attributed revenue: how many tracked clicks it drove, how many turned into sales, and the total dollars matched to it.

Two lists do most of the work:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Top earners" icon="trophy">
    Your posts ranked by revenue, best first. This answers the only question that matters: **what should I post more of?**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dead posts" icon="circle-xmark">
    Posts that got clicks but earned nothing — content that looked like it worked and didn't. This tells you what to **stop** making.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Read the deeper guide: [Revenue & top earners](/guides/revenue-and-top-earners).

## Money Feed — the live activity stream

The **Money Feed** is the newest-first stream of what's happening right now — clicks, sales, and refunds as they land, each tagged with the platform and country it came from. It's the view to leave open on launch day: you watch a post go out and see the clicks, then the sales, roll in. seenpaid also raises **alerts** here for sales that land and posts that fail to publish. See [Money Feed & alerts](/guides/money-feed).

## Money DNA — why your posts earn

Once a few posts have earned, **Money DNA** finds the patterns behind them: your best-earning platform, the caption length that pays, whether links and media help *you specifically*, and your best day of week. It's built only from posts that actually earned, so it stays quiet (and says so) until there's enough signal. See [Money DNA](/guides/money-dna).

## Analytics — where the clicks came from

Beyond totals, **Analytics** breaks your traffic down by country, device, referrer, browser, and UTM campaign, and shows the clicks → leads → sales funnel. It's how you learn which source actually converts, not just which gets clicks. See [Analytics](/guides/analytics).

## Attribution health — can you trust the numbers?

**Revenue → Attribution health** shows what share of your real Stripe revenue seenpaid could trace back to a post. This is the honesty check: a low number means your tracking setup has a gap (usually the pixel), not that your posts failed. Always glance here before reading a low revenue figure as bad news.

## What to do with all this

A simple weekly loop:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the briefing">
    Look at top earners, dead posts, and attribution health for the last 7 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Double down">
    [Recycle](/guides/recycle) or repost your top earner so a proven winner keeps working.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cut the losers">
    Stop making the kind of post that shows up under dead posts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the next idea">
    Run a revenue-decided [experiment](/guides/experiments) on your next hook.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Prefer to just ask? Connect an AI agent over MCP and say *"Give me a briefing on the last 7 days and tell me what to do next."* The agent reads all of the above and answers in one message. See [seenpaid for AI agents](/agents/overview).
</Tip>
