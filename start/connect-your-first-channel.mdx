A **channel** is a social account seenpaid can publish to. You only need one to get going. This step walks you through connecting the platforms that connect most easily, and tells you honestly which ones need extra setup or aren't quite ready.

<Info>
  Not sure which platform to start with? Pick **LinkedIn** or **Bluesky** — they connect in a couple of clicks and both let a tracked link live right in the post, so you'll see attribution working fastest.
</Info>

## The basic flow

Every platform follows the same three clicks. The differences (below) are only in what each platform asks of you along the way.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channels">
    In the left rail, click **Channels** (this is where connected accounts live).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a platform and click Connect">
    Choose the network and click **Connect**. seenpaid sends you to that platform's own login to authorize — you're approving seenpaid on the platform's site, not typing a password into seenpaid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and come back">
    Approve access. The platform sends you back to seenpaid, and the account appears in your channel list with an **active** status. It's now available in the composer and to your AI agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  "Done" for this step is one account showing **active** under Channels. That's all you need to publish your first post.
</Check>

## Platform by platform

### LinkedIn — connect directly

Click **Connect** on LinkedIn, sign in to LinkedIn if prompted, and approve. You can publish to a personal profile. Captions can be long (up to 3,000 characters) and a link in the post body is tracked automatically — a great first channel.

### Bluesky — connect directly

Click **Connect** on Bluesky and authorize with an app password. Captions are short (300 characters), and links in the post are tracked. Fast to set up and friendly to attribution.

### Mastodon — connect directly

Click **Connect** on Mastodon. Because Mastodon is federated, you'll confirm your home server (instance) as part of authorizing. Captions are 500 characters and in-post links are tracked.

### Pinterest — connect directly

Click **Connect** on Pinterest and approve. Pinterest **requires an image** to create a Pin — you'll add media when you compose. In-post links are tracked.

<Warning>
  **X (Twitter):** X charges for API posting on your own developer account (their pricing, not ours). To publish to X you register your own X developer app and paste those keys into seenpaid — see [Bring your own keys](#bring-your-own-keys). Without credits on your X account, a publish attempt will fail on X's side.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **Facebook, Instagram and Threads** publish through Meta's Graph API, which requires Meta app review. They're wired into seenpaid and will light up once that review clears. Until then, start with the directly-connectable platforms above. We'd rather tell you this plainly than have a connect button that quietly fails.
</Note>

## Bring your own keys (BYOK)

Some networks — notably **X** — gate API access behind a developer app you register on their side. For those, seenpaid supports **bring-your-own-keys**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register a developer app on the platform">
    Create an app in that platform's developer portal and copy the API credentials it gives you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste them into seenpaid">
    In **Channels**, choose the platform, and paste the keys when prompted. seenpaid stores them and publishes on your behalf.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dashboard tells you exactly when a platform needs this — if it asks for API keys, that's the BYOK path.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A platform won't let me connect">
    A few networks require you to register a developer app first (the BYOK path). If the dashboard prompts for API keys, follow the platform's steps, paste the keys, and connect. For Facebook/Instagram/Threads, the connection is pending Meta's app review — use a directly-connectable platform for now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My account connected but shows as 'needs reconnecting'">
    Access tokens can expire or be revoked by the platform. Open **Channels**, find the account, and click **Reconnect** to re-authorize. An account in this state won't publish until it's reconnected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I approved on the platform but nothing came back">
    Pop-up blockers and third-party-cookie settings can interrupt the return trip. Try again in a normal (non-incognito) window, and allow pop-ups for seenpaid.com.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
