A **channel** is a social account seenpaid can publish to. You only need **one** to get going. This page shows you the two ways platforms connect, tells you honestly which are ready today, and gets your first account to **active** in a couple of minutes.

<Info>
  **In a hurry?** Start with **LinkedIn**. One click, no keys to copy, captions up to 3,000 characters, and a link right in the post gets tracked — so you'll see attribution working fastest. Then come back for the rest.
</Info>

## The two ways a platform connects

You never register seenpaid anywhere. What changes per network is **where the permission comes from** — and that's decided by the platform, not by us. Every channel is one of these:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Click &amp; authorize" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square">
    Click **Connect**, sign in on the platform's own site, approve, and you're bounced back. Nothing to copy or paste.

    **LinkedIn · Mastodon · Pinterest · Tumblr**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paste your own key" icon="key">
    The network gives *you* a credential — an app password, API key, bot token, or webhook URL — which you paste in once. Nothing for us to register on our side.

    **Bluesky · Discord · Telegram · Slack · WordPress · Ghost · Dev.to · Hashnode · Medium · Lemmy · Nostr · Webhook**
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  **Coming a little later:** Facebook, Instagram, Threads and TikTok publish through APIs that require platform review (in progress). **X** connects with your own keys but needs your own paid X API credits. **Reddit and VK** are coming soon. The dashboard shows each platform's real state — a channel you can't finish yet is never a live button that quietly fails.
</Note>

## Method A — click &amp; authorize

The fastest path. Use it for **LinkedIn, Mastodon, Pinterest, or Tumblr**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channels">
    In the left rail, click **Channels** — this is where connected accounts live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a platform and click Connect">
    Choose the network and click **Connect**. seenpaid sends you to that platform's own login to authorize. You're approving seenpaid on the platform's site — never typing a platform password into seenpaid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and come back">
    Approve access. The platform returns you to seenpaid and the account appears under Channels with an **active** status — ready in the composer and for your AI agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  "Done" for this step is **one account showing active** under Channels. That's all you need to publish your first post.
</Check>

## Method B — paste your own key

For **Bluesky, Discord, Telegram, Slack, WordPress, Ghost, Dev.to, Hashnode, Medium, Lemmy, Nostr, and Webhook**, the network gives you a credential to paste. It sounds technical; in practice it's copy-one-value-and-paste.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the credential from the platform">
    Each network keeps this in its own settings — see the per-platform notes just below for exactly where. You copy one value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the platform's card in Channels and paste">
    Click the platform in **Channels**, paste the value into the field it shows, and confirm. seenpaid stores it encrypted and publishes on your behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm active">
    The account turns **active** and joins your channel list, exactly like an authorized one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Bluesky — app password" icon="cloud">
    In Bluesky: **Settings → App Passwords → Add App Password**. Copy it and paste it with your handle. Use an app password, **not** your main password — it's revocable on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Discord — webhook URL" icon="discord">
    In your Discord server: **Channel settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy Webhook URL**. Posts arrive as messages in that channel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Telegram — bot token" icon="telegram">
    Message **@BotFather**, create a bot, copy the token it gives you, and add the bot to your channel/group as an admin so it can post.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Slack, WordPress, Ghost, Dev.to, Hashnode, Medium, Lemmy" icon="key">
    Each hands you a credential in its own developer/settings area — an incoming webhook (Slack), an application password (WordPress), an Admin API key (Ghost), or a personal API token (Dev.to, Hashnode, Medium, Lemmy). The card in Channels names the exact field. Paste and confirm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Nostr — private key, or Webhook — a URL" icon="bolt">
    Nostr publishes with your signing key (nsec). Webhook is the escape hatch: give any HTTPS endpoint and seenpaid POSTs your post's content to it — wire it into anything you run yourself.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Which should I connect first?

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Fastest to attribution" icon="bolt">
    **LinkedIn** or **Bluesky** — both let a tracked link live right in the post, so a click is attributable the moment you publish. Best for seeing the money loop close today.
  </Card>
  <Card title="You already live there" icon="heart">
    Connect wherever your audience actually is. seenpaid attributes the same way on every Live channel — the "best" first channel is the one you'll really post to.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  You can connect **as many accounts as your plan allows**, across as many platforms as you like — a personal LinkedIn and three client LinkedIns all count as separate channels. Start with one; add the rest whenever.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A platform I want isn't showing a Connect button">
    It's one of the not-yet-available ones: Facebook, Instagram, Threads and TikTok are pending platform review; Reddit and VK are coming soon; X needs your own paid API credits. Start with any Live channel — the rest light up automatically as they clear.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My account connected but shows 'needs reconnecting'">
    Access tokens can expire or be revoked by the platform. Open **Channels**, find the account, and click **Reconnect** to re-authorize. An account in this state won't publish until it's reconnected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I approved on the platform but nothing came back">
    Pop-up blockers and strict third-party-cookie settings can interrupt the return trip. Try again in a normal (non-incognito) window and allow pop-ups for seenpaid.com.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It's asking for a key and I don't have one">
    That's the paste-your-own-key path — the platform issues the key, not seenpaid. Follow the per-platform note above to grab it (it's one value), then paste it back in Channels.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
