---

Once seenpaid is connected over [MCP](/agents/mcp), you drive it in plain language — the agent picks the right tool. These are prompts that map cleanly onto the [47 tools](/agents/tools-reference), grouped by intent. Say them to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or any MCP client with your key configured.

<Info>
  You don't name tools. "Which post made the most money?" is enough — the agent calls `get_top_posts` on its own. The groupings below just show which tools each prompt tends to reach for.
</Info>

## See what earned

The questions no other scheduler can answer.

- "Which of my posts made the most money this month?"
- "Which platform made me the most money this month?"
- "Show me my top 5 posts by revenue."
- "Which posts got clicks but earned nothing?"
- "Which channel actually returns the most per post?"
- "How's my revenue this week compared to last?"
- "Is my revenue trending up — which day spiked?"
- "How much has this specific post earned?"
- "What just happened on my account — any sales in the last hour?"

_Reaches for `get_top_posts`, `get_channel_roi`, `get_dead_posts`, `get_analytics`, `get_analytics_timeseries`, `get_post_revenue`, `get_money_feed`._

## Get a briefing

When you want the whole picture in one answer instead of five.

- "Give me a briefing on the last 7 days."
- "How are we doing this month?"
- "What should I do next, based on what's actually earning?"
- "Anything broken, and anything worth reposting?"

_Reaches for `summarize_performance`, which rolls up earnings, dead posts, broken channels, and recommended actions._

## Publish & schedule

- "Post 'New drop is live: mystore.com' to X and LinkedIn right now."
- "Schedule that for tomorrow at 9am on X and LinkedIn."
- "Draft a post about my latest launch and schedule it for tomorrow at 9am."
- "Plan me a week of posts and schedule them all."
- "Will this caption post cleanly to X, LinkedIn and Instagram?"
- "Move my Friday post to Saturday 10am."
- "Cancel the post scheduled for tonight."
- "Make an image of a laptop and coffee in warm light, then attach it to a post about my morning routine."
- "Give me a tracked link for this post to drop in my YouTube description."

_Reaches for `schedule_post`, `bulk_schedule`, `validate_post`, `update_post`, `cancel_post`, `generate_image`, `create_tracked_link`._

## Forecast & time it

- "Forecast this caption, then schedule it if it beats my average."
- "Which of these two captions is likely to earn more?"
- "What do my best-earning posts have in common?"
- "When should I post next for the best shot at a sale?"

_Reaches for `forecast_post`, `get_money_dna`, `get_next_slot`._

## Double down on winners

- "Repost my top earner for next Tuesday."
- "Find my highest-earning post and run it again with a fresh link."
- "Auto-recycle any post that earned over $100, at most once every 14 days."

_Reaches for `get_top_posts` → `repost`, and `configure_recycle`._

## Run it on autopilot

- "Turn on autopilot: 3 drafts every 4 days about indie SaaS growth, for X and LinkedIn."
- "Show me the autopilot drafts waiting for approval."
- "Approve the best draft and schedule it for Tuesday 9am."
- "Generate a fresh batch of drafts now."

_Reaches for `configure_autopilot`, `list_suggestions`, `approve_suggestion`, `generate_suggestions`._

## Test what works

- "A/B test three hooks for my launch post on X."
- "Launch that experiment."
- "Which variant won, and can you reuse the winning caption?"

_Reaches for `create_experiment`, `launch_experiment`, `get_experiment_winner`._

## Keep the account healthy

- "Which social accounts do I have connected?"
- "Is anything broken — did a channel stop publishing?"
- "How many more channels can I connect on my plan?"
- "Give me a link to connect my LinkedIn."
- "What share of my Stripe sales is being traced to a post?"
- "Any new alerts I should know about?"

_Reaches for `list_accounts`, `get_account_health`, `get_plan_limits`, `get_connect_url`, `get_attribution_health`, `list_notifications`._

<Tip>
  A good opening move for any new account is simply: **"Give me a briefing on the last 7 days and tell me what to do next."** It surfaces top earners, dead posts, broken channels, and your attribution match rate in one shot.
</Tip>
