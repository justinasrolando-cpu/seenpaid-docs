The seenpaid MCP server (`seenpaid` v1.0.0) exposes five tools. All are scoped to the organization behind your API key.

## `list_accounts`

List the connected social accounts. Use the returned `id` or `platform` values when scheduling a post.

**Input:** none

**Returns:** an array of `{ id, platform, name, status }`.

## `list_posts`

List recent posts with their status, schedule time, and target platforms.

<ParamField path="limit" type="number">
  Max posts to return, 1–50. Defaults to 20.
</ParamField>

**Returns:** an array of `{ id, caption, status, scheduledFor, publishedAt, platforms }`.

## `schedule_post`

Schedule or immediately publish a post to one or more connected platforms.

<ParamField path="caption" type="string" required>
  The post text (1–3000 characters).
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="platforms" type="string[]">
  Which platforms to post to, e.g. `["x", "linkedin"]`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="account_ids" type="string[]">
  Specific connected-account ids (from `list_accounts`). Overrides `platforms`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="schedule_for" type="string">
  ISO-8601 time to publish. Must be in the future. Omit to publish immediately.
</ParamField>

<Note>
  Provide `caption` plus either `account_ids` or `platforms`. Omit both to post to **every active account**.
</Note>

**Returns:** `{ ok, postId, status, scheduledFor, postedTo }`.

## `get_analytics`

Get revenue-attribution analytics — clicks, sales, and revenue, with per-platform, per-country, and per-device breakdowns — over a window.

<ParamField path="days" type="number">
  Window in days: `7`, `30`, or `90`. Defaults to `30`.
</ParamField>

**Returns:** `{ window_days, currency, totals, by_platform, top_countries, by_device }`.

## `get_top_posts`

Get your posts ranked by revenue earned. This answers "which post made the most money."

**Input:** none

**Returns:** up to 10 posts as `{ caption, status, clicks, sales, revenueCents, currency }`.

---

<Tip>
  Revenue is returned in **cents** (`revenueCents`) alongside a `currency` code — divide by 100 to display dollars. Attribution requires a connected Stripe account and at least one published tracked post.
</Tip>
